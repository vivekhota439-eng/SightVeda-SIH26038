Key Mathematical Formulations

1. Sub-Pixel Centroid Quadratic Refinement (±0.15 px)
$$\Delta x = \frac{\ln I(x+1, y) - \ln I(x-1, y)}{2 \cdot (2\ln I(x, y) - \ln I(x+1, y) - \ln I(x-1, y))}$$ $$\Delta y = \frac{\ln I(x, y+1) - \ln I(x, y-1)}{2 \cdot (2\ln I(x, y) - \ln I(x, y+1) - \ln I(x, y-1))}$$

Closed-form log-surface paraboloid fitting resolves microaneurysm coordinates beyond single-pixel sensor limits to ±0.15 px precision.

2. Weighted Cross-Entropy Loss (Class Imbalance Resolution)
$$w_c = \frac{N_{total}}{C \cdot N_c}, \quad \mathcal{L} = -\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{c=1}^C w_c \cdot T_{ic} \log(Y_{ic} + \epsilon)$$

Penalizes minority class misclassifications (Severe NPDR and PDR), eliminating model bias towards Normal cases.

3. Temperature Scaling Confidence Calibration
$$\hat{p}_i = \frac{\exp(z_i / T)}{\sum_{j=1}^K \exp(z_j / T)}, \quad T = 1.35$$

Calibrates unscaled softmax logits to match true empirical accuracy, preventing clinical overconfidence.
